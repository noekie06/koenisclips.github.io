Privacy policy

KoenisClips uploader · last updated 12 November 2026

What this app is

KoenisClips uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the KoenisClips channels. It uploads short gaming videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no other users and does not offer accounts or sign-ups.

YouTube API Services

This app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's basic information. By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is used

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's own YouTube account, stored only on the owner's private server and used only to upload videos and read the owner's channel information.
  • Video files and titles created by the app itself.

The app does not collect, store or share personal data of other people. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.
